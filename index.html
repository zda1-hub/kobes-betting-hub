🚨 BETTING HUB DISCLOSURE

Bet with intention.

Before you place any bets using picks from the Betting Hub, understand that you are ultimately responsible for your own bets and your own money.

I will never send a pick that I personally don’t believe in. Every pick I send is something I have confidence in and have done my research on. But at the end of the day, we are betting on sports. Nothing is guaranteed.

If you choose to tail a pick, do it because YOU are comfortable with the bet and the amount of money you are putting down. Never blindly follow a pick just because I sent it.

Most importantly, NEVER bet money you cannot afford to lose. The best mentality to have when betting is to treat the money you are putting down as if you have already lost it. If losing that money would cause you stress, affect your bills, or negatively impact your life, DO NOT BET IT.

Bet responsibly, stay disciplined, and remember that this is supposed to be something you enjoy—not something that puts you in a bad financial situation.

I’m here to give you my best picks, research, and analysis. What you do with them is ultimately your decision. 🫡

📈 WHY THE PICKS WORK

High hit rate. Strategic betting.

The goal behind my picks isn’t to throw out random bets and hope they hit. It’s about high hit rates + strategic betting.

I put a lot of time into finding picks that I genuinely believe have an edge. When you consistently hit at a high rate—around 70% over a large sample size—that creates a huge advantage over time.

But the other half of the equation is how you manage your bankroll.

You can have great picks and still lose money if you’re betting recklessly. That’s why I always recommend watching your units, bankroll, and bet sizing. Don’t chase losses, don’t randomly increase your bets because you’re confident, and don’t let one bad day change your entire strategy.

High hit rate + disciplined unit management + long-term consistency = the potential for long-term profit. 📈

We’re not trying to get rich off one pick or one night. We’re trying to build a sustainable strategy where the small edges add up over hundreds and thousands of bets.

Trust the process. Stay disciplined. Protect your bankroll. Let the numbers work over time. 🫡

Even with a high hit rate, there will always be losing streaks. Nothing in sports betting is guaranteed.
