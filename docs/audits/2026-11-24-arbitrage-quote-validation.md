# Arbitrage quote validation

Requested change: hold implausible, mismatched, or stale arbitrage quotes before an approval card or VIP post. The Nov 24 Eagles @ Cowboys report paired Dallas decimal 2.88 with an opposing quote, producing about 55.13% total implied probability. Dallas's reported actual -475 is about 1.2105 decimal. Those numbers are incident inputs from the owner, not a fresh feed verification.

Affected files: `bot/lib/arbitrage-paper-monitor.js`, its tests, `scripts/arb-alert-dry-run.js`, and the npm script. Before, selected quotes could be five minutes old and there was no implied-total or 5% maximum-edge guard. Approval re-fetched odds but could publish at changed prices. After, a candidate must be an upcoming event with two exact team outcomes in standard `h2h` markets, selected quotes at most 60 seconds old, selected-side implied total 90–102%, and edge at most 5%. At approval the original card must still be fresh; a new fetch must return the same game, market, books, teams, and prices. Both approval and VIP cards display the selected-side implied total and each quote's age. Rejections are logged with reason codes.

Verification: `node --test bot/lib/arbitrage-paper-monitor.test.js` covers the Nov 24 pair, a corrected -475 decimal comparison, low/high implied total, over-5% edge, stale quotes, wrong market, live event, moved price, and no VIP publication. `npm run arb:dry-run` prints the rejection reasons from historical/illustrative fixtures and makes no network or Discord calls. The full repository suite has eight unrelated failures from other test files; the focused arb suite passes. This branch is local/PR only and is not deployed.
