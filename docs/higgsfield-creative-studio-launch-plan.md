# Higgsfield Creative Studio Launch Plan

## The offer

Create short-form ad packages for businesses that need stronger Meta, Instagram, and TikTok creative but do not have an in-house production team. Kobe's Betting Hub can be the first case study, but the studio should also serve lower-restriction categories such as local services, ecommerce, fitness, food, and software.

## Production workflow

1. Client completes a brief covering the offer, audience, proof, brand rules, and prohibited claims.
2. Build three hooks and two scripts for each concept.
3. Generate visual scenes in Higgsfield using approved reference assets.
4. Human-review every frame for false text, brand errors, invented products, and misleading results.
5. Edit captions, audio, disclosures, and calls to action.
6. Export 9:16, 4:5, and 1:1 variants with safe-zone checks.
7. Client approves the exact final files before launch.
8. Measure hook rate, hold rate, click-through rate, checkout rate, and paid conversion—not views alone.

## First productized packages

- **Pilot:** 3 concepts, 2 hooks each, 6 short videos, one revision round.
- **Growth:** 8 concepts per month, 3 hooks each, weekly delivery and performance review.
- **Creative testing:** a recurring batch of new hooks and iterations based on actual ad results.

Price only after measuring Higgsfield credit use, editing time, revision time, and delivery costs. Keep API/credit overages in the agreement and never promise campaign revenue.

## Kobe's Betting Hub pilot

- Education-first hook: “A pick is more than a team—it is a line, price, and timestamp.”
- Organization hook: “Too many betting feeds? See one reviewed board.”
- Arbitrage hook: “Two books can briefly disagree. Here is what must happen before the price moves.”
- Proof format: show the product interface and complete, qualified records; do not fabricate slips or imply typical profits.
- Required language: 21+ where permitted, sports betting involves risk, no outcome or profit is guaranteed.

## Tracking gate before meaningful ad spend

Do not scale based on clicks alone. The Meta Pixel currently measures page views and checkout starts. Connect a confirmed successful Stripe payment to Meta's Purchase event (preferably server-side through Conversions API), deduplicate browser/server events, and test it before increasing spend.

## Compliance gate

Real-money gambling and related advertising can require Meta's prior written permission and location/age restrictions. Obtain the required account authorization before launching betting-related campaigns. Avoid “guaranteed wins,” “risk-free profit,” fake urgency, fabricated testimonials, and claims that a specific return is expected.
