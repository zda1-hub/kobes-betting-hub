FAQ

Clear answers, up front.

What to expect from membership, releases, recaps, access, and risk.

Membership

What does membership include?

Membership provides access to the private Discord channels and member releases described on the membership page. The public site and free picks remain available to everyone.

How does billing work?

Choose the offer shown on the membership page. After any stated introductory period, the subscription renews at the displayed monthly price until canceled.

Can I cancel?

Open Manage membership from the top-left menu using the secure Stripe checkout confirmation link. Cancellation stops the next renewal, while paid Discord access continues through the period already paid for.

Releases and results

When are releases posted?

Timing varies by slate and market. Each post includes its timing and bet details when available.

How many plays will I receive?

There is no guaranteed daily number. The goal is qualified releases, not filling a quota.

Are all results recapped?

The standard is to include every official posted play, including wins, losses, and pushes.

Risk and access

Are picks guaranteed?

No. No pick or system guarantees profit. Sports betting involves real financial risk.

Does the Hub place bets for me?

No. It provides information and community access and never needs your sportsbook password.
