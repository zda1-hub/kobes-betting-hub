OFFICIAL SUPPORT

Need membership help?

Get help with Discord access, billing questions, or canceling a membership through the official Support channel or support email.

Use the official Support channel.

Do not send card details, passwords, login codes, crypto, or identity documents in Discord messages. Kobe's Betting Hub will never ask for them.

Cancel or manage membership

Use Manage membership, sign in with the Discord account linked to the membership, and continue to Stripe’s secure customer portal. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues through Stripe’s displayed paid-through date.

Manage membership →

Membership charges are non-refundable except where required by applicable law, card-network rules, or a written Hub exception. Cancellation prevents the next renewal; it does not retroactively refund the introductory payment, current month, or a partially used period.

Do not post your checkout email, card information, receipt number, or any other billing details publicly in the channel.

Discord access or billing question

Open #support or email support@kobesbettinghub.com and describe the issue without sensitive information. The team can then move the conversation to a private, verified support path if account details are needed.

Support hours: Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM Pacific Time. Expect a response within one business day. Messages received outside support hours are reviewed during the next support window.

Urgent gambling help

If gambling is causing distress or harm, stop wagering and visit the responsible gambling page. For an immediate crisis, contact local emergency services.
