How membership, Discord access, and referral payouts use information.

DRAFT • BUSINESS REVIEW REQUIRED • SEPTEMBER 12, 2026

Service providers are connected.

Stripe processes checkout and payout information, Discord supplies member identity and access, Supabase stores operating records, and Cloudflare hosts the website and service.

Information used

The service may process technical logs, Discord user ID and verified email, Stripe customer and subscription references, membership status, referral attribution and reward status, and support records. Bank and card details are entered with Stripe and are not stored by the Hub.

Why it is used

Information is used to provide member access, manage subscriptions and cancellations, prevent duplicate or self-referrals, verify reward eligibility, send cash payouts, investigate errors or abuse, and maintain security and audit records.

Payments and payouts

Card and payout details should be entered only on Stripe's secure pages. Stripe may collect identity, bank, and tax information required to send payouts.

Cookies and sharing

The site does not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate membership, Discord access, hosting, persistence, and payouts. Essential security and session technologies may be used.

Retention, security, and contact

Operational and financial records are retained as needed for service, security, dispute, tax, and legal purposes. The final policy will add precise retention periods, the business identity, mailing address, jurisdiction, and privacy contact.
