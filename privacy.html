Privacy notice.

How membership, Discord access, and referral payouts use information.

EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 13, 2026 • PUBLIC BETA

Who operates this service

Kobe Irwin operates Kobe's Betting Hub from California. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to support@kobesbettinghub.com.

Information used

The service may process your Discord user ID, Discord username and verified email, an email address you voluntarily enter to receive free picks and updates, Stripe customer and subscription references, membership and cancellation status, referral attribution and reward status, support communications, IP address, browser and device information, and security and API audit records. Card, bank, identity-verification, and payout details are entered directly with Stripe and are not stored by the Hub.

First-party website analytics records browsing sessions, pages viewed, foreground time, scroll depth, and coarse click positions to understand site usability. It does not record typed text or form values for this purpose. The private dashboard shows these measures in aggregate; the click map currently covers the join page.

Why it is used

Information is used to provide and secure member access, manage subscriptions and cancellations, synchronize Stripe status with Discord roles, prevent duplicate or self-referrals, verify reward eligibility, send eligible payouts, answer support requests, investigate errors or abuse, reconcile billing, and maintain operational and financial records. If you opt in through the website, we use your email to send a welcome message, free picks, and occasional Hub updates. Every list email includes an unsubscribe link.

Service providers, advertising, and sharing

Stripe processes checkout, subscriptions, invoices, billing-portal activity, and referral payout onboarding. Discord supplies member identity and community access. Supabase stores membership and audit records. Cloudflare hosts the website and membership service and stores email-list signups. Resend sends opted-in emails from support@kobesbettinghub.com. When you allow optional tracking, public marketing and checkout pages use Google Analytics to measure page visits and interactions, and the Meta Pixel, which may send Meta browser and device information, page visits, checkout-intent events, and Stripe-verified purchase events with the amount paid and currency for advertising measurement, attribution, audience building, and campaign optimization. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services here. The Hub does not send card or bank information through these tags and does not sell personal information for money.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed to provide the service and support, reconcile payments and referrals, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, secure the platform, and satisfy tax, accounting, and legal obligations. Retention can vary by record type. Access is restricted, secrets are kept outside public website code, and payment credentials remain with Stripe; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your tracking choice

Optional site analytics and advertising tags run only after you allow them. You can change your choice at any time.

Your choices and requests

You can manage billing and cancellation through the Stripe customer portal. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information controlled by the Hub by emailing support@kobesbettinghub.com. Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, or legal compliance. The Hub will verify account ownership before disclosing or changing account information.

California privacy and advertising choices

California residents may use the same support address to ask which categories of personal information the Hub has collected, request correction or deletion where applicable, ask questions about privacy practices, or request that eligible browser-based advertising disclosures stop. You can also use browser privacy controls and Meta’s advertising settings. The Hub does not sell personal information for money.

Age restriction

The service is intended only for people of legal betting age where they live and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact
